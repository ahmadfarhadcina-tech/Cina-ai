Privacy Policy

This page explains how Cina AI is designed to handle information when you use our website, dashboard, and API.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Cina AI is being developed as an independent AI platform for developers. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of information that may be collected and how that information is intended to be handled.

Because the platform is still under development, some services described below may not yet be active. Actual collection and processing will depend on the features available at the time you use Cina AI.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Cina AI may collect information such as:

  • Account name and email address.
  • Authentication and account security information.
  • API key metadata and key usage information.
  • API request metadata such as endpoint, timestamp, status, and usage.
  • Information you voluntarily provide through support or contact forms.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the platform.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to provide, maintain, secure, and improve Cina AI services.

  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Authenticate and manage API requests.
  • Measure API usage and enforce service limits.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Maintain service reliability and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Communicate important service or security information.

3. API data

When you use the Cina AI API, your application may send prompts, messages, instructions, and other content to our API infrastructure.

The exact handling of API input and output data will depend on the deployed Cina AI model and the applicable service configuration. Before production API processing is enabled, the platform should clearly document applicable data retention and model-training practices.

Important

Cina AI should not be used to submit passwords, payment credentials, private keys, or other highly sensitive information unless the applicable service documentation explicitly states that such data is supported.

4. Security

Cina AI is designed with security controls including authenticated API access, protected account credentials, rate limiting, usage controls, and secure handling of API keys.

Production API keys are intended to be displayed in full only when they are created. The backend should store a secure representation of keys rather than recoverable plaintext secrets.

5. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security, meet operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Specific retention periods for API requests, logs, usage records, and account information will be documented as those production systems are implemented.

6. Data sharing

Cina AI is intended to minimize unnecessary disclosure of user information. Information may be disclosed when needed to operate the service, protect users and infrastructure, comply with legal obligations, or respond to legitimate security requirements.

Cina AI will not claim that information is never shared unless the applicable production service and its legal arrangements actually support that statement.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control certain information.

Requests can be handled through the contact method provided by Cina AI once the production account and support systems are active.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Cina AI develops new features, changes its infrastructure, or becomes subject to additional legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

Questions regarding privacy or data handling can be directed through the official Cina AI support channel once it is published.
